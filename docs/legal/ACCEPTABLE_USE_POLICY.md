# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** March 2026  
**Applies To:** All users of HeyGetOnMyLevel

---

## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy defines the permitted and prohibited uses of HeyGetOnMyLevel ("the Service"). By using the Service, you agree to comply with this policy.

---

## 2. Permitted Uses

The Service is intended for:

- ✅ Reading comprehension practice at any grade level
- ✅ Phonics learning and sound-letter correspondence practice
- ✅ Vocabulary building and word recognition
- ✅ Educational assessment and level identification
- ✅ Classroom supplemental use by educators
- ✅ Parent-child shared reading activities
- ✅ Adult literacy improvement
- ✅ English language learning support

---

## 3. Prohibited Uses

Users shall NOT:

### 3.1 Security & System Integrity
- Attempt unauthorized access to any part of the Service
- Interfere with or disrupt servers, networks, or infrastructure
- Introduce malware, viruses, or harmful code
- Attempt to reverse engineer, decompile, or extract source code
- Bypass or circumvent security measures
- Perform vulnerability scanning without written authorization

### 3.2 Abuse & Misuse
- Use automated systems, bots, or scripts to access the Service
- Generate excessive load intended to degrade performance (DoS)
- Use the Service for any unlawful purpose
- Exploit the AI content generation for non-educational purposes
- Systematically scrape or harvest generated content

### 3.3 Content & Communication
- Submit personal information (your own or others') through feedback forms
- Submit abusive, threatening, or harassing content through feedback
- Attempt to manipulate AI content generation to produce inappropriate material
- Use the Service to generate content for commercial purposes without permission

### 3.4 Data & Privacy
- Attempt to identify or de-anonymize other users
- Collect or harvest any information about other sessions
- Use the Service to process, store, or transmit personal data of third parties

---

## 4. Feedback System Rules

When using the feedback mechanism:

| Rule | Requirement |
|------|------------|
| No PII | Do not include names, emails, phone numbers, or addresses |
| Character limit | Messages limited to 500 characters |
| Appropriate content | Feedback should be constructive and relevant |
| No spam | Do not submit repeated or automated feedback |

---

## 5. Educator and Institutional Use

Educators and institutions using the Service should:

- Supervise student use, especially for younger learners
- Not require students to submit personal information through the Service
- Understand that the Service does not track individual student progress
- Use the Service as a supplement to, not replacement for, formal curriculum
- Acknowledge the AI content disclaimer in the Terms of Service

---

## 6. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in:

1. **Warning:** For first-time minor violations
2. **Temporary restriction:** For repeated or moderate violations
3. **Permanent blocking:** For severe or persistent violations

We reserve the right to take action without prior notice for severe violations that threaten system integrity or user safety.

---

## 7. Reporting Violations

If you become aware of a violation of this policy, please report it using the in-app feedback button. Do not include personal information in your report.

---

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to address new use cases or threats. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
