## 2. COPPA Compliance Analysis

### 2.1 COPPA Applicability

The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act applies to:
- Commercial websites and online services directed to children under 13
- General audience sites with actual knowledge of collecting personal information from children under 13

### 2.2 Our Status

| COPPA Requirement | Our Status | Explanation |
|-------------------|------------|-------------|
| Operator of child-directed site | Potentially | Educational content for grades K-8 |
| Collects personal information | No | No personal data collected from anyone |
| COPPA obligations triggered | No | No collection = no consent required |

### 2.3 Definition of Personal Information Under COPPA

COPPA defines "personal information" as:

| COPPA Category | Collected? | Details |
|----------------|------------|---------|
| Full name | ❌ No | Not requested or stored |
| Physical address | ❌ No | Not requested or stored |
| Email address | ❌ No | Not requested or stored |
| Phone number | ❌ No | Not requested or stored |
| Social Security number | ❌ No | Not requested or stored |
| Persistent identifier for behavioral tracking | ❌ No | Session ID is ephemeral, not persistent |
| Photo, video, or audio of child | ❌ No | No media collection* |
| Geolocation | ❌ No | Not requested or stored |
| Screen name | ❌ No | No accounts or usernames |

*Speech recognition runs locally in browser; no audio transmitted or stored.

### 2.4 COPPA Safe Harbor

While we do not collect personal information and therefore COPPA consent requirements do not apply, our practices align with COPPA Safe Harbor principles:

- Data minimization
- Purpose limitation
- Transparency
- Security measures

## 4. Data Practices for Children

### 4.1 What We Don't Do

We explicitly DO NOT:

- ❌ Collect personal information from children
- ❌ Require account creation
- ❌ Ask for parent/guardian information
- ❌ Use persistent identifiers for tracking
- ❌ Enable child-to-child communication
- ❌ Display behavioral advertising
- ❌ Sell or share children's data
- ❌ Create profiles of child users
- ❌ Use gamification to extend engagement
- ❌ Send push notifications
- ❌ Collect location data

### 4.2 What We Do

We DO:

- ✅ Generate age-appropriate educational content
- ✅ Use ephemeral session identifiers
- ✅ Delete all session data when browser closes
- ✅ Provide immediate feedback on practice
- ✅ Allow use without any personal information
- ✅ Display privacy warnings on feedback form
- ✅ Limit feedback submissions to 500 characters

## 5. Feedback Form Protections

### 5.1 Risk Mitigation

The feedback feature presents a potential vector for children to share personal information. We mitigate this through:

| Protection | Implementation |
|------------|----------------|
| Warning message | "Please don't include personal info" displayed prominently |
| Character limit | 500 character maximum |
| No required fields | No name/email/contact requested |
| No storage | Messages sent via email only, not database |
| No response | One-way communication only |

### 5.2 Recommended Parental Guidance

For the feedback feature, we recommend parents:
- Supervise children when using the feedback form
- Remind children not to share personal information
- Review feedback messages before submission
