## Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how HeyGetOnMyLevel ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our reading practice application (the "Service").

## Our Commitment to Privacy

HeyGetOnMyLevel is designed with privacy at its core. We believe in providing a valuable educational tool without requiring personal information. This policy explains what minimal data we handle and how we protect your privacy.

---

## What We Collect

We collect only what's necessary for the app to function:

- **Session ID:** A random identifier (UUID) that exists only while you use the app. This is not tied to your identity in any way.

- **Reading Level Selection:** The grade level you choose to practice (e.g., Grade 3, Grade 5).

- **Practice Responses:** Your answers during reading sessions. These are used only for immediate feedback and are not graded or stored permanently.

- **Assessment Results:** If you take an optional reading assessment, the results are used only to suggest an appropriate reading level and are stored only for your current session.

- **Anonymous Geographic Data:** When you use AI-powered features (reading passages, assessments, tutoring), we record the country, region, and city derived from CDN infrastructure headers (Cloudflare). This data is aggregated as daily counts (e.g., "10 requests from US, California, Los Angeles on April 10"). No IP addresses are stored or logged. This data is used solely for operator traffic monitoring and cost management.

- **Aggregated Usage Counts:** We track daily counts of AI feature usage by type (e.g., "15 passage requests, 8 tutor requests on April 10"). No individual session or user is identifiable from this data.

---

## What We Don't Collect

We intentionally do not collect:

- Names, email addresses, or contact information
- Age, birthdate, or demographic information
- Location data or IP addresses for tracking (note: anonymous country/region/city from CDN headers is collected in aggregate only — see "What We Collect")
- Device identifiers or fingerprints
- Browsing history or behavior across other sites
- Any information that could identify you personally
- Voice recordings (speech recognition, when used, runs entirely in your browser)

**We do not sell, share, or rent any information to third parties.** This applies to all users, regardless of location.

---

## How Long We Keep Data

Your session data is **ephemeral** — it exists only while you're actively using the app. When you close your browser or navigate away, your session is automatically deleted. We don't maintain any persistent records of your reading practice.

Specifically:
- Session data is deleted when your browser session ends
- We do not create user profiles
- We do not maintain reading history
- We do not track progress over time

## Third-Party Services

We use the following services to provide our functionality:

### AI Content Generation

Reading passages and comprehension questions are generated using artificial intelligence. No personal information is sent to these services — only requests for educational content at a specific grade level.

### Feedback Delivery

If you choose to submit feedback through our feedback form, your message is sent via email to our team. Important notes:
- We do not store feedback submissions in any database
- We display a warning asking you not to include personal information
- Feedback is limited to 500 characters

### Hosting

The application is hosted on Lovable Cloud, which provides secure infrastructure for the Service.

---

## Cookies

We use only one cookie: a **sidebar preference cookie** that remembers whether you've collapsed the navigation sidebar. This is a strictly functional cookie that:

- Does not track you
- Does not contain personal information
- Is not shared with any third parties
- Is not required for the app to function

No consent banner is required for this type of cookie under privacy regulations including GDPR and ePrivacy Directive.

## Children's Privacy

HeyGetOnMyLevel is open to users of all ages, including children. Our privacy practices are designed with young users in mind:

### Why We Don't Need Parental Consent

Because we don't collect any personal information from anyone (including children), the special consent requirements under COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar regulations do not apply.

### Our Child-Safe Practices

- No registration or account creation required
- No collection of names, ages, or contact information
- No social features or user-to-user communication
- No advertisements or third-party tracking
- AI-generated content is designed to be age-appropriate

### Guidance for Parents

We encourage parents and guardians to:
- Supervise their children's online activities
- Remind children not to share personal information in feedback forms
- Use the application together with their children for a shared learning experience

## Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal information, traditional data rights (access, correction, deletion) don't apply in the usual sense. However, you can always:

- **Leave the app at any time** — your session data is automatically deleted
- **Use the app without providing any personal information** — this is the only way to use the app
- **Contact us with any privacy questions or concerns** — email privacy@time-2-read.com

### For EU/EEA Residents

Under GDPR, you have rights regarding personal data. Because we don't collect personal data, these rights are satisfied by default:
- Right to access: No personal data exists to access
- Right to rectification: No personal data exists to correct
- Right to erasure: No personal data is stored
- Right to data portability: No personal data to transfer

### For California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

Under CCPA/CPRA, you have specific rights. Our practices satisfy these:
- Right to know: This policy discloses all data practices
- Right to delete: No personal information is stored
- Right to opt-out of sale: **We do not sell or share any information**
- Right to non-discrimination: Service is free for everyone
- Right to limit use of sensitive information: No sensitive information collected

### For Residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, and Other U.S. States

State privacy laws (VCDPA, CPA, CTDPA, and others) provide similar rights to CCPA. Because we do not collect personal information, these rights are satisfied by default:
- Right to access, correct, and delete: No personal data exists
- Right to opt out of targeted advertising: No advertising conducted
- Right to opt out of sale: No data sold or shared
- Right to appeal: Contact us at privacy@time-2-read.com

### For Brazilian Residents (LGPD)

Under the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, our practices comply because no personal data is collected. The same privacy-by-design principles that satisfy GDPR also satisfy LGPD requirements.

### For Canadian Residents (PIPEDA)

Our transparency and consent practices meet PIPEDA requirements. Since no personal information is collected, consent obligations are satisfied by design.
